# Setting up your account

!!! info

    **Last updated:** August 26, 2026

## How to get an editorial account in OJS 

**If your journal is new to YDJ**, YDJ will invite one or more managing editors to create an account in OJS. This will give your editorial team give initial access to the system.

**If your journal has already been established with YDJ**, your predecessor should create your account and manage your permissions. YDJ will only get involved if there are extenuating circumstances (e.g., if prior editors cannot be located). 

In either case, the editors are responsible for creating editorial accounts and managing permissions. Starting in OJS 3.5, editors no longer have the ability to create or edit user accounts outright; instead, editors [invite new users and invite existing users to new roles](what-to-expect-in-ojs-35#inviting-users).

!!! info

     Unless you configure OJS otherwise, users can create accounts and assign themselves the role of Author and Reviewer without editor involvement. 

## Accessing your profile 

From the front-end view of OJS, you can access your profile settings by clicking your username in the farthest top right corner of the screen, then clicking _View Profile_. 

From the dashboard view of OJS, you can access your profile settings by clicking the silhouette icon in the farthest top right corner of the screen, then clicking _Edit Profile_. 

You have limited access to your account’s roles through the profile. To view and edit the roles associated with your account, use the _Users & Roles_ settings. 

## Managing notifications 

OJS can send a lot of notifications. Some are important but some may be extraneous. 

To do this, [access your profile (per the instructions above)](#accessing-your-profile) and open the _Notifications_ tab. As an editor, you probably want to keep all the Public Announcements, and Reviewing Events, and Submission Events. However, Statistics Report Summary is not necessary.  

!!! warning

    If you select “Do not send me an email for these types of notifications,” notifications will only be displayed in OJS. YDJ does not advise using this option.  

## Connecting your ORCID ID  

You can connect your [ORCID ID](https://orcid.org/) to your account. This means that anything you author with your account (ie, the Contributor record for the submission is pre-populated with information from your account) or peer review will link to your ORCID ID account.  

To do this, [access your profile (per the instructions above)](#accessing-your-profile) and open the _Identity_ tab. Click the button that says _Create or Connect your ORCID ID_. A pop-up window will appear, asking you to log in to your ORCID account to authorize the connection.
